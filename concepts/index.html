How should the game enter the essay?

All three concepts borrow AI 2040’s editorial language: warm paper, book typography, restrained colour, large chronological headings and marginal evidence. They differ in how strongly the board interrupts the reading experience.

A

Margin Chronicle

The closest match to AI 2040. The essay remains primary; annotated board plates appear like consequential figures in a long-form report.

B

Cinematic Interlude

The board takes over the whole viewport at important moments. A compact evidence card and the timeline float over the map.

Open concept
C

Living Essay

A scrollytelling interpretation: prose advances down one side while a sticky board changes state beside it.
